Compares two reconstruction modes per level top=0..3:
  - generate: self-generated at every level AND every AR digit timestep (real inference path).
  - teacher_force: real label_fn-targeted encode, decode-cascade ctx CHAINS level i's own predicted
    code (argmax of one parallel teacher-forced pardec_score call) down to level i-1, but every
    digit-level AR step within a level is teacher-forced against the real target (never self-fed).
    Prints a per-level agree_with_gt trace (fraction of codes matching ground truth at that level)
    to localize where the cascade diverges.

=== interpretation ===
teacher_force_mse stays nearly flat across depth (58 -> 66 -> 63 -> 66, val) even though per-level
code agreement with ground truth is low at the top (top=3's own level: 2.9% val, barely above
1/256=0.39% chance) -- lower levels' decoders recover gracefully from an imperfect upstream code
when their OWN digit-level AR steps are teacher-forced. generate_mse instead degrades severely and
monotonically with depth (2.8x -> 30x worse than teacher_force). Since training only ever exercises
pardec_score (parallel teacher-forced) and never pardec_generate's own sequential digit-by-digit
self-feeding, this points at within-level digit-AR generation (never trained via its own sampling
procedure) as the dominant compounding-error source -- not primarily cross-level code propagation,
which this probe shows is comparatively robust.
