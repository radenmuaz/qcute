=== Bug found (initial diagnostic, before this audit) ===
dense_self_attention_pardec had `extra = real_query & tail_pos`, letting real (loss-computing)
Kspan queries attend into decode_future's tail region -- which embeds REAL ground-truth tokens
from the NEXT group. Ablating just that term on the trained checkpoint:
  acc_with_leak (original code) = 0.9990
  acc_no_leak   (extra removed) = 0.1374
  drop = 0.8617
This explained the symptom: near-perfect teacher-forced accuracy, near-garbage free-running
generation (which structurally never had access to decode_future's tail).

=== Fix applied ===
Removed real_query/tail_pos/own_chain_key entirely from dense_self_attention_pardec /
run_block_pardec / decode_logits_and_target_pardec. Plain causal masking over the whole widened
(draft+real+tail) per-group sequence is sufficient and correct: tail sits chronologically after
real positions, so standard causal masking already excludes it from real queries at every layer --
no custom masking was ever needed. The old own_chain_key block was only required to plug a
transitive path that the `extra` term itself created; removing `extra` makes it unnecessary too.

=== BEFORE/AFTER on the (already-fixed) checkpoint ===
BEFORE (old leaky mask reconstructed and applied to the fixed checkpoint's weights):
  dense TF acc vs real = 0.0850
AFTER (current fixed mask, matches how the checkpoint was actually trained):
  dense TF acc vs real = 0.7796  (level0), 0.7253 (level1)
Accuracy DROPS under the old leaky mask here (opposite direction from the original bug demo)
because these weights were trained under the fixed (causal-only) regime -- forcing the old mask
on them is now the mismatch. Confirms training and current code are mutually consistent.
